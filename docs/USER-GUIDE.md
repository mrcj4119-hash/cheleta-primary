# User Guide

## Sign in

Select your role and sign in with your school account. Parents can select **Create an account** on the sign-in screen to register. Local demonstration accounts are listed in the repository README. Signing out clears the browser's session token.

## Admin

- Use **Learners** to register a learner and update a profile.
- Use **Teachers** to create a teacher profile, sign-in account, and class assignments or edit the profile.
- Use **Staff accounts** to create student sign-ins and review all system accounts. Teacher accounts are added under **Teachers**. Parents register their own accounts and are linked to learners by an administrator.
- Use **Learning resources** to publish subject notes, career-readiness resources, coding bootcamp material, and revision questions for all grades or one grade.
- Use **Mentorship** to publish and close programs.
- Use **Student suggestions** to review student ideas and update their status.
- The dashboard shows school progress and parent comments for review.
- Use **Assessments** to enter CBC records and edit assessment history.
- Use **Attendance** to record or correct daily attendance.
- Use **Timetable** and **Lesson planning** to publish class schedules and follow lesson progress.
- Use **Report cards** to generate term reports with CBC results, attendance, and teacher comments; use **Print / Save as PDF** to download a copy.
- Use **Notifications** to publish parent announcements and review your own alerts.
- Use **Wellbeing & safeguarding** to record and follow up on confidential learner-support cases. Access is limited to admins and teachers assigned to the learner's class and is audited.
- Use **Library & inventory** to maintain book, supply, and equipment counts and issue or return books.
- Use **School reports** to review attendance patterns, class progress by term, and learners needing follow-up.
- Use **Data exports** for role-scoped CSV files. **Data backup** downloads a SQLite backup and is admin-only; store it securely.

## Teacher

Use **Learners** to view your assigned classes. Administrators manage learner records and student sign-ins. Create CBC assessment records, review parent comments for assigned classes, and record attendance for assigned classes. Add timetables and lesson plans for assigned classes, update lesson progress, prepare report cards, and record or follow up on confidential learner-support cases for your assigned classes. Learning resources, mentorship, and class schedules are also available.

## Parent

Create an account from the sign-in screen. A school administrator will link your account to your learner before records are available. Use **My learner**, **Learning progress**, **Attendance**, **Timetable**, and **Report cards** to view records for linked learners. Parents may submit one non-editable comment per linked learner. In **Notifications**, add an international phone number and explicitly opt in if you want SMS alerts; SMS is sent only after the school configures its provider account.

## Student

Use the school-provided student sign-in to access your linked learner profile, assessments, and attendance. **Learning library** contains subject notes, revision questions and answers, career-readiness notes, and coding bootcamp activities matched to your grade. **Mentorship** lists eligible programs you can join. Use **Suggestion box** to send an idea directly to the school admin and track its status.

## Board of Management

Board accounts see school-wide totals and class-level progress, including aggregate attendance, assessment, and mentorship information. Individual learner identities and records are not available to Board accounts.

## School staff

Staff accounts can review school learner, attendance, and assessment records and access learning resources and mentorship information. Editing or account management remains restricted to authorized roles.

## Data reminder

All included records are fictional demo data. The prototype is not approved for real learner information. Safeguarding records and backup files are especially sensitive; do not enter real data or store backups on shared devices.
